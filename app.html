🎷 Jazz Harmony Guide

Welcome to Jazz Harmony PRO. Generate professional chord progressions instantly.

1. Quick Start

  • Select a Key (e.g. C, Bb, Eb).
  • Choose a Style/Template (Standards, Neo-Soul, Lo-Fi).
  • Click GENERATE.

2. Controls

  • PLAY ALL: Listen to the full sequence with real piano audio.
  • VOICING MODE: Switch between BASIC (Theory view) and JAZZ (Professional Shell Voicings).
  • BASS: Toggle left-hand bass for practice.

3. Library

Click ❤️ SAVE to store your favorite progressions locally. Export as MIDI to use in your DAW.
